# IMP-015: Deduplicate Xfail Documentation Between Tests/Readme And PBS Regressions

## Status

Proposed

## Layer

04-verification

## Theme

The same information about the two strict xfail markers (`signal_sine_gain_add/baseline`, `dcmotor/baseline`) appears in `tests/README.md`, `product-breakdown/04-verification/regressions.md`, and `product-breakdown/04-verification/decisions/VD-002.md`. Consolidate to the structured regression tracker and cross-link from the test README.

## Evidence

1. `tests/README.md`, lines 85-89: Describes the two xfail variants, their purpose, and the XPASS detection policy.
2. `product-breakdown/04-verification/regressions.md`, lines 11-14: Structured REG-001 and REG-002 entries with root cause, status, and workaround.
3. `product-breakdown/04-verification/decisions/VD-002.md`: Decision record documenting the choice to use strict xfail markers as the tracking method.

The pytest xfail markers themselves (in `test_reference_ssps.py`, lines 234 and 273) are the mechanism — they stay as-is.

## Current Pain Or Risk

- **Triple narrative**: The same xfail status is described in three places. When REG-001 is fixed, all three must be updated.
- **tests/README.md drifts toward product description**: The test README exists to help developers run and understand tests, not to track regression status. The structured tracker in PBS is the right place.

## Proposed Improvement

1. Keep the canonical regression documentation in `product-breakdown/04-verification/regressions.md` (structured with IDs, root cause, status, workaround).
2. Keep `product-breakdown/04-verification/decisions/VD-002.md` as-is (it documents the decision to use xfail markers — that is a stable architecture decision).
3. In `tests/README.md`, replace the xfail paragraph (lines 85-89) with a brief cross-link: "Known regression fixtures are tracked in `product-breakdown/04-verification/regressions.md`."

## Expected Benefit

- Single structured tracker for known regressions.
- tests/README.md stays focused on test layout and commands.

## Risk And Blast Radius

- **Blast radius**: 2 files (tests/README.md and regressions.md). ~5 lines removed from tests/README.md.
- **Risk**: Low. Text changes only.

## Suggested Priority

Low — existing duplication is stable and well-understood.
