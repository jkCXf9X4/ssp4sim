# Remove the recorder from the executor

**Question:** Should the recorder be removed from the executor and instead injected into the models earlier?

**Evidence:** A raw developer note states the executor's recorder is no longer needed and that the recorder should be injected into the models earlier in the pipeline.

**Change:** Move recorder injection out of the executor into model construction, and rewire all consumers that reach the recorder through the executor.

**Risk:** Unknown — the note is unmeasured; recorder consumers and the construction path must be audited before the move.

**Acceptance:** Models receive the recorder at construction; the executor no longer owns one; recording behavior and tests are unchanged.
