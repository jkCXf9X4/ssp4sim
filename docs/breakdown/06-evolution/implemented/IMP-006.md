# Reduce docs/overview.md to a pure landing page with PBS links

**Question:** Should `docs/overview.md` stop partially duplicating product positioning and act only as a landing page into the product layer?

**Evidence:** The overview reproduces product positioning ("a small experimental SSP simulation engine...") that the product-layer capabilities, scope, and glossary leaves already own, creating a second narrative and a drift point.

**Change:** Reduce the overview's descriptive paragraphs to a single purpose sentence and links to the product-layer capabilities, scope, and glossary; keep the existing cross-reference.

**Risk:** Very low — one file, ~6 lines removed; positioning text is already present in the product layer and readme.

**Acceptance:** The overview is a clean entry page with links and no competing product description.
